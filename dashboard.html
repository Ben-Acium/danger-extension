Danger Extension
Every permission from the internal risk catalog, each wired to a real call against your own browser. Nothing leaves this machine. ChromeOS-only / enterprise-managed APIs are marked unavailable rather than faked.

Overview

This extension is loaded with the full permission catalog you use to grade extension risk — Critical, High, Medium, and Standard — so you can see exactly what each one puts within reach, using your own browser, on your own machine.

Nothing here calls out to a network endpoint. Every panel reads local browser state on demand when you click a button. A few permissions (ChromeOS device APIs, enterprise-managed APIs, platform-app-only APIs) won't do anything outside a managed ChromeOS environment — those panels say so plainly instead of faking a result.

Pick a permission from the left to see what it exposes. Use the filter box to jump straight to one.
